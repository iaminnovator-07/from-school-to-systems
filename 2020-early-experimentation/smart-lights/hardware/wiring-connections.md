# 🔌 Wiring Connections

## LDR Sensor

| LDR Pin | Arduino Connection |
|---|---|
| VCC | 5V |
| GND | GND |
| OUT | A0 |

---

## IR Sensor

| IR Sensor Pin | Arduino Connection |
|---|---|
| VCC | 5V |
| GND | GND |
| OUT | D2 |

---

## LEDs

| LED | Arduino Pin |
|---|---|
| LED 1 | D8 |
| LED 2 | D9 |
| LED 3 | D10 |

Negative terminals connected to GND through resistors.
