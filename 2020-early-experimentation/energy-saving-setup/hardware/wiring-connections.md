# 🔌 Wiring Connections

## LDR Sensor

| Sensor Pin | Arduino Connection |
|---|---|
| VCC | 5V |
| GND | GND |
| OUT | A0 |

---

## Relay Module

| Relay Pin | Arduino Connection |
|---|---|
| VCC | 5V |
| GND | GND |
| IN | D8 |

---

## LED Indicator

| LED | Arduino Pin |
|---|---|
| Status LED | D9 |

Negative terminal connected to GND through resistor.
