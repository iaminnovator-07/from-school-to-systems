## Purpose of Components

### Arduino UNO
Main embedded controller handling automation logic.

### LDR Sensor
Detects surrounding brightness levels.

### Relay Module
Controls connected electrical devices automatically.

### LEDs
Provide automation and power-status indication.

### Breadboard & Jumper Wires
Used for hardware prototyping and circuit setup.
