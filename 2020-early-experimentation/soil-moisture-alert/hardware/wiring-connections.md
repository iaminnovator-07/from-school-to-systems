# 🔌 Wiring Connections

## Soil Moisture Sensor

| Sensor Pin | Arduino Connection |
|---|---|
| VCC | 5V |
| GND | GND |
| OUT | A0 |

---

## Buzzer Module

| Buzzer Pin | Arduino Connection |
|---|---|
| Positive | D8 |
| Negative | GND |

---

## LED Indicator

| LED | Arduino Pin |

| Alert LED | D9 |

Negative terminal connected to GND through resistor.
