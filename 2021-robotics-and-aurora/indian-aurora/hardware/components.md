• PAM8403 Audio Amplifier  
• Bluetooth Module

---

## Purpose of Components

### Arduino UNO
Main embedded controller handling interaction and automation logic.

### PAM8403 Amplifier
Amplifies audio output for louder and clearer speaker sound.

### Bluetooth Module
Enables wireless communication and app interaction.

### SD Card Module
Stores educational audio files and interaction responses.

### Rechargeable Batteries
Provides portable power supply for the system.

### Charging Port Module
Allows battery charging and portable operation.

### Speaker Module
Outputs educational voice and interaction audio.

### 3D Printed Structure
Physical upgraded body structure of Indian Aurora.
